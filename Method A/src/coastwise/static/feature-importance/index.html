How importance is measured

Increase in held-out mean absolute error when a feature group is shuffled. Values near zero or below show little evidence of contribution.

How correlations are measured

Spearman rank correlation with severity, over the rows where the feature is recorded. 95% intervals come from resampling whole bents.
