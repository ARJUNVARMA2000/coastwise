
Additional audited contract (takes precedence):
- Review every paragraph in <pages> before reading the context. Return page_review with the
  absolute body page number, status observations or no_observations, and a concise reason.
  Include page_review even when components is empty.
  A page with documented condition observations or repaired damage still has observations.
  A page of recommendations without observed findings may have no_observations.
- Copy an explicit defect-specific number, code or label into severity as written. Preserve case.
  A severity range stays one raw value. Leave severity NR for an unrated defect even when a
  component or section has a condition such as Fair or Typical. Repair/Renew marks are actions.
- Use rating_scope section only for a named area-wide condition and component for a physical item.
  Give severity_scale the cited verbatim scale name only when the report connects that scale to
  that rating. Similar wording by itself is insufficient. Keep unknown scales NR.
- Copy source-supported material, material_grade and structural_role with tags on the applicable
  component or defect. A shape or size such as HP12x84 is not an ASTM grade. Do not infer grades
  or load-bearing importance. Use NR for an unstated property.
- Each findings_text must be one contiguous exact passage. Cite every block of a passage that
  wraps across adjacent source lines belonging to the same paragraph or row. Use context_quotes
  for independent snippets such as headers, units, scoped notes or a second mention. Never
  join unrelated cells into a sentence. Cite both the item label and the damage cell.
- Table blocks may arrive out of visual order. When present, x and vertical y ranges identify
  left/right columns and alignment. A vertically merged rating applies to its enclosed row
  group only. A heading can apply to multiple rows; disambiguate repeated row labels using their
  parent (e.g. which bay's connection) in identifier. Keep each distinct location.
  Cite the actual row remarks for findings, the physical item or group heading for its name,
  and the shared unit header for its unit. Never use a note about one row to fill a neighboring
  row. A typical-condition paragraph remains group context unless the report ties it to the item.
  Group all dimensions and readings of a single physical defect/location into one finding.
  Report a shared group rating once with its own scope. Attach the relevant readings as
  separate measurements. A measurement subrow is not another defect.
  At the top of a continued table, check the prior page's last group before assigning rows
  to the first group named on the body page. A merged label can be centered below its first
  row. In narrative, cite the active face or area heading, including a heading on the previous
  page. Preserve a shared condition at the original scope.
- Each measurement has measurement_kind: coating_loss, thickness_loss, section_loss,
  remaining_thickness or other. Distinguish steel substrate thickness from coating or cross-section
  area. unit_src can cite a different tag for a shared unit header. Keep property specific
  (e.g. outer flange remaining thickness). Retain raw numbers, inequality and range text.
  For a compound dimension such as 3”x9”, retain the full raw value and cite the inch symbol
  in unit and unit_src. Do not emit separate confirmed defects for alternatives in one statement.
  Cite the confirmed finding and keep the alternative wording as context.
- Historical or repaired findings are allowed when the earlier damage itself is stated. Keep
  their status and time context. Do not invent damage from a repair or replacement alone.
- <source_context> is supplemental text to interpret <pages>. It can support scoped facts and
  definitions but cannot create a component observation or defect on its own. Keep the primary
  findings_text and rating/measurement values anchored to the rows you extract in <pages>.
- Check every observed condition acknowledged in page_review against the returned rows. Explicit
  irregular levels/alignment and historical condition statements must not disappear. Keep a
  historical finding historical, including its year, whether members were actually inspected,
  exceptions, original rating ranges and stated basis. A present observation followed by a
  conditional rehabilitation recommendation remains a present observation.
- Cite the governing physical-element heading for material, and supplemental construction text
  only when its subject unambiguously matches this component/section. Preserve reinforcement,
  slab thickness and running-surface role as context; slab thickness is not damage depth. Retain
  an explicit affected-member total when it refers to the same finding. Geometry words such as
  shallow, wide or deep are not severity ratings without an explicitly applicable rating scale.
